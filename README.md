 🛠️ My Approach

I don't believe you need expensive hardware, expensive APIs, or a massive budget to experiment with serious technology.

Sometimes you just need:

an idea + a stubborn developer + Wi-Fi + a laptop that refuses to die. 😂

 📚 Documenting the Journey

I believe the process matters as much as the final product.

So I document the ideas, architecture, experiments, failures, fixes, decisions, and lessons behind what I build.

Because if something works but nobody knows how or why, that's only half the story.
